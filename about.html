About CareTrack

Caring for mothers and children, building a healthier tomorrow.

CareTrack is a maternal and child healthcare management system designed to make healthcare simple, accessible, and more efficient for every family.

Our Mission

To empower mothers and caregivers by providing a smart and reliable platform that helps them manage appointments, vaccinations, follow-ups, and clinic visits with ease.

Our Vision

To be the leading digital solution for maternal and child healthcare, ensuring every mother and child receives the best care at the right time.

What We Do

CareTrack brings together essential healthcare services in one place to support mothers, children, and healthcare providers.

Appointment Tracking

Easily schedule and manage prenatal and postnatal appointments.

Vaccination Management

Track your child's vaccination schedule and receive timely reminders.

Queue Monitoring

Check real-time clinic queues and estimated waiting times before your turn.

Follow-ups & Reminders

Get notified about upcoming appointments, overdue visits, and important follow-ups.
